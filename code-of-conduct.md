The hall is open to anyone who builds, tests or writes about agents. These rules keep it that way.

## Members

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the build, not the builder.
- Report what you ran. Never present an unrun test as passing or a borrowed number as yours.
- Credit sources. Link the repository you built on and keep its licence.
- No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, no unsolicited promotion.

## Maintainers and Assayers

- Review the head that exists, not the description of it.
- Never approve your own build, your own session's work, or a build you have a financial interest in without saying so.
- Publish every reproduction, including the ones that disagree with the author.
- Decline work you cannot verify.

## Builds

- A build must say what it refuses to do, list every hook and script, and carry a licence.
- A build must not phone home, exfiltrate files, or install anything beyond what its page lists.
- A build that is found doing so is delisted and the finding is published.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and suspend members. Decisions are posted on Discussions with the reason. Appeals go to guild@guildbuild.ca.
