These are promises, written before the first tier is earned so that nobody has to take our word for it later.

## No pay-to-rank

Tiers are computed from evidence files committed to a public repository. There is no field a maintainer can set. Sponsored and verified labels are visually distinct from tiers and never change a build's position.

## Evidence stays whole

Failed and incomplete runs stay in the denominator. Records with different environment fingerprints are never averaged. A record, once accepted, is not edited; a correction is a new record that references it.

## Your files stay yours

Public downloads keep working. If a paid plan ever closes, private metadata exports before any retention deadline. Builds and guides are Apache-2.0 unless a page says otherwise; you keep your copyright.

## Nothing runs on our server

The site renders metadata and links. It never executes a contributed build, fetches a contributed URL at render time, or runs a trial on your behalf. Hosted runs, if they open, run in isolated runners with a cost cap and a separate authorisation.

## Honest status

A module that is not public says so. A build without evidence says Declared. A season that covers one runtime says one runtime. "Coming" means coming, not done.

## No surprise charges

If a paid plan ever hits a limit, the action pauses and you choose: export or upgrade. No overage after the fact.

## Privacy is not an upgrade

Basic privacy is free for every member. The site sets no tracking cookies, loads nothing from third parties, and stores an email only when you type one into a form.
